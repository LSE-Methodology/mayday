Phase 3

Optimise for Production

~30 minutes — A model that's too slow is a model that doesn't get used.

1

Understand the penalty

The simulation has a latency budget (shown on the dashboard settings). If your model takes longer than the budget to score a batch, there's a probability that request gets a random ad instead of your model's pick. The slower you are, the more traffic you waste.

Check the "Timeouts" column on the leaderboard. If it's not zero, you're leaving revenue on the table.
2

Benchmark before you upload

Use the benchmark_latency() function in the notebook. It measures inference time on a batch of 10 rows (the same batch size the simulation uses). Look at the P95, not just the median — one slow call in twenty still hurts.

Logistic regression is typically under 1ms. A 200-tree LightGBM model is usually under 5ms. Where does yours sit?
3

Ways to speed things up

If your model is too slow, you have options:

  • Fewer trees or a shallower max depth
  • Drop low-importance features (check feature_importances_)
  • Use a simpler model for part of the pipeline
  • A fast model that predicts roughly the right thing beats a slow model that predicts precisely the right thing
4

This is a real tradeoff

In production ad systems, models have strict latency SLAs — typically 10–50ms. Teams routinely sacrifice a few percentage points of accuracy to stay within budget. There's no right answer; it depends on how much accuracy you lose vs how much traffic you save.

If your model is well within budget, don't optimise further. Spend the time improving your target or features instead.
