The Scenario
An e-commerce ad problem

You have access to a database of 500,000 ad impressions across 10,000 users and 200 ad creatives, complete with click and purchase data.

Your team's goal: build a model that decides which ads to show to which users in order to generate the most value. You'll deploy it to a live simulation and see how different approaches compare on a shared dashboard.

The metric is revenue per impression. Sounds straightforward — but the data has structure worth exploring first. The obvious approach may not be the best one.

How It Works
Four phases, start to finish

Each phase builds on the last. You'll go from open-ended data exploration to a model running against live traffic.

Explore ~45 min

Query the database through a web-based SQL editor or directly from your notebook. Get to know the users, ads, and funnel — and look for patterns that might shape your modelling choices.

Build ~75 min

Pull training data, engineer features, and train an ML model. The most interesting decision isn't which algorithm to use — it's deciding what your model should optimise for.

Optimise ~30 min

Your model needs to serve predictions in real time. Profile its latency and think about the tradeoff between complexity and speed — a practical constraint you'd face in any production system.

Test Live ~45 min

Upload your model to the shared simulation. A live dashboard shows how each team's approach performs on the same stream of traffic — a good way to compare strategies and discuss what worked.
